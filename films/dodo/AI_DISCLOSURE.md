# AI disclosure: The Dodo Came Back

## Extent of AI use
Category B (AI). All imagery in the film was generated by AI (Muse Image) from prompts written by an AI director (Muse Spark). Animation is procedural (camera moves, depth layers, puppet bends), computed by the Cutaway software and rendered in Blender; sound is openly licensed recordings and synthesized ambience, chosen per shot by Muse Spark.

## Human and AI authorship
- The story idea is the author's own (a dodo wakes in modern Port Louis, becomes an attraction and ends in an enclosure with many dodos). The written brief (story.md) was drafted from that idea with an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) and approved by the author.
- The Cutaway software was written with the same AI assistant.
- Choices that overruled the models (locking keyframes, overruling critiques, rewriting prompts) are recorded below as Director revisions. In this production they were made by the AI assistant acting on the author's behalf, under the author's instruction to keep working autonomously; the author reviews them before submission.
