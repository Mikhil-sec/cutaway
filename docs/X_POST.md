What if the dodo came back to Mauritius? 🦤

I built Cutaway for the Meta Global AI Hackathon: an AI film crew that doesn't generate video. It *builds* it.

• Muse Spark plans the shots
• Muse Image paints them from real Port Louis photos
• SAM 3.1 cuts them into 3D layers
• Blender films them
• Muse Spark and Muse Wonder watch the result and make it again, better

150 seconds, one consistent dodo, a laptop, a few dollars.

Open source: github.com/Mikhil-sec/cutaway
