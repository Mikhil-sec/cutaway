# MFDC National Short Film Competition 2026: the rules that matter for us

A summary of the contest rules PDF the user shared on 2026-11-01. The full Terms & Conditions are a separate document, which we haven't seen yet.

## Our entry

- **Category B (Artificial Intelligence).** Theme 3: **"What If the Dodo Returns?"**
- **Duration: 90 to 180 seconds.**
- Category B has no AI percentage cap: "AI may be used as permitted under the Competition Rules". Categories A and C are capped at 25%.
- **One film only per participant.** Category and theme must be stated at submission.
- **Deadline: 13 November 2026, 4:00 p.m. Mauritius time**, through MFDC's official submission method.
- Open to participants from the Republic of Mauritius. MFDC may ask for proof of identity or residence.

## What the film must meet

- It must be in the required format, accessible and playable. **The rules state no resolution or file format**; check the official submission page. We deliver 1920×1080 H.264 MP4 regardless.
- It must be **produced after the competition launched.**
- **Rights to everything:** music, sound, photos, footage, fonts, locations, identifiable persons, and **AI-generated material**.
  - The use of the friend's Port Louis photos needs their permission.
  - The video model's output licence must allow this use. fal lists Wan 3.0 (`alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video`) as `license_type: commercial` (checked 2026-11-01); record that in CREDITS.md and the disclosure.
- **AI disclosure:**
  - the percentage or approximate extent of AI use;
  - the tools and platforms used;
  - which elements are AI-generated or AI-assisted;
  - how AI contributed.
- **MFDC may ask for source files, prompts, generation records, metadata or editing timelines.** `generation_log.jsonl` and `films/dodo/generation_records.jsonl` hold these; keep every video-model call logged too.
- Misleading or missing disclosure means disqualification.

## Judging criteria

Creativity and originality; storytelling; relevance to the theme; direction and visual execution; cinematography; editing and sound; cultural relevance; technical quality; effective and responsible use of AI.

## Prizes (Category B)

Rs.75,000, Rs.25,000 and Rs.10,000. Participants keep their IP, and MFDC gets a licence for administration, judging, archival and promotion.
